# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary real audience: course evaluators reviewing a cross-platform app development project. They judge the app on first look: flows must read clearly without instruction, every screen must demonstrate a complete feature (auth, dashboards, chat, payment).

In-app fiction: everyday buyers posting product requests and small sellers browsing, responding, chatting and meeting. Buyers arrive with a concrete need and want responses fast; sellers scan a feed and decide where their offer fits.

## Product Purpose

A reverse marketplace prototype: buyers post what they want, sellers view, chat and meet. Success is a demo that walks end to end (signup → role dashboard → post/respond → chat → mock payment → success/failure states) with zero dead ends and no explanation needed.

## Brand Personality

Bold and distinctive. Nature-derived poise from the Steller's Jay: charcoal crest, structural blues, one amber spark. Bold contrast, calm motion, crisp copy. Confidence like Stripe (brand color used with conviction, financial-grade clarity) with the identity-driven color energy of Figma. Emotions: trust, clarity, a quiet sense of character.

## Anti-references

- Dated 2015 web: Bootstrap-era gradients, glossy bevel buttons, clip-art icons, heavy borders, dense banners.
- AI-slop SaaS: sparkle icons, purple-blue gradients, identical glass cards, generic hero metrics, decorative blur.
- Classifieds clutter: shouting all-caps, badge spam, banner ads, visual noise.

## Design Principles

1. First-look legibility: an evaluator must understand any screen in under three seconds, without narration.
2. Character through structure, not decoration: the Steller's Jay shows up as charcoal-dominant headers, one committed mark, and disciplined color, never as clip-art or ornament.
3. Earned familiarity: standard product affordances (top bar, form vocabulary, chips, list rows) stay predictable; surprise is reserved for the crest mark and moments of success.
4. One vocabulary everywhere: the same buttons, fields, radii, and states across all ten screens, including success/failure and payment.
5. Complete states ship: every interactive element has default, focus, disabled, loading, error, and an empty state that teaches the next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: all text ≥ 4.5:1 against its background, tap targets ≥ 44px, status never conveyed by color alone (icon or label alongside), motion state-only and reduced-motion friendly, form errors explicit and adjacent to fields.
